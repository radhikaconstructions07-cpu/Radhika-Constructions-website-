About Us

Experience grounded in the field.

Radhika Construction & Surveying combines practical civil engineering experience with modern surveying and mapping techniques.

Radhika Construction & Surveying

Established in 2000, Radhika Construction & Surveying is a Jabalpur-based civil engineering and surveying practice providing services for residential, commercial and infrastructure-related requirements.

Our work focuses on accurate site understanding, surveying, mapping, planning and practical engineering support. Modern tools and field-based methods are used according to project requirements.

From land surveys and mapping to building planning and construction-related work, our objective is to provide dependable technical information for real project decisions.
