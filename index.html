What We Do

Professional Services

A range of civil engineering, surveying, mapping and planning services designed for residential, commercial and infrastructure requirements.

01 / MAPPING

Aerial Mapping & GIS Survey

Drone-based aerial information and GIS-oriented mapping for understanding site conditions and land features.

02 / LAND

Land Survey & Contours

Detailed field surveying and contour information to support site assessment, design and planning.

03 / DEMARCATION

Plot & Land Demarcation

Practical demarcation work to establish and communicate site boundaries based on available survey information.

04 / ROADS

Road Surveying & Levelling

Survey and level information for road and infrastructure related work, supporting accurate site execution.

05 / TERRAIN

Catchment Area Analysis

Terrain and drainage-related analysis to help understand how water moves across a site and its surrounding area.

06 / BUILD

Construction & Planning

Engineering-oriented support for construction planning, site understanding and project preparation.

07 / APPROVALS

T&CP and RERA Approved

Town & Country Planning (T&CP) and RERA approved planning and layout services supported by accurate survey information.
