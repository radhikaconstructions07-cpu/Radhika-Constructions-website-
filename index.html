About the firm

Technical work, handled with a practical mindset.

Radhika Construction & Surveying provides land surveying, mapping, planning and construction-related support for clients who need dependable field information before moving ahead with a project.

Our work combines engineering understanding with tools such as Drone, GIS, DGPS, Total Station and Auto Level to help turn site conditions into useful information for planning and execution.
