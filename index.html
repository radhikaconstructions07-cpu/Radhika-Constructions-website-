How a project moves forward

Every project begins on the ground and ends in an approved document — in that order.

Survey

02

Analysis

03

Planning

Site plans, layouts and civil design developed around T&CP and RERA requirements.

04

Documentation

Drawings, reports and approvals prepared and carried through to execution on site.
